Our Methodology

The Agentic Research Loop

01

Decomposition

The system analyzes the core intent of your inquiry. It doesn't just search for keywords; it maps the intellectual landscape, identifying key debates, seminal papers, and emerging trends that need to be addressed.

02

Active Retrieval

Using secure APIs and advanced web-crawling technology, ScholarAI accesses proprietary and public academic databases simultaneously. It evaluates source credibility on-the-fly, prioritizing high-impact factor journals and verified institutional repositories.

03

Dialectical Synthesis

Finally, the system reconciles conflicting data points. It highlights areas of consensus and divergence, creating a nuanced synthesis that reflects the true complexity of the subject matter, complete with in-line citations.
